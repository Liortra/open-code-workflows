# Feature: Recipe Catalog

The product provides a catalog of recipes spanning a handful of categories
(Breakfast, Main, Side, Dessert). It lets a user browse the catalog and view
an individual recipe's details — its title, category, ingredient list, and
step-by-step instructions. This browsing/viewing capability is the entry
point users rely on to select a recipe for Cook Mode or to assign to the
Meal Planner.
