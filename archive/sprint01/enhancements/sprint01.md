# Sprint 01 — Study Aids & Progress Tracking

a. **English Text-to-Speech** — speak the English meaning aloud too, not just
   the Hebrew word, so Study mode works as a listen-and-recall drill in both
   directions.

b. **Spaced Repetition (SRS)** — track per-word recall history and surface a
   "Due for Review" queue across all lessons, instead of always drilling a
   full lesson start to finish.

c. **Progress Dashboard** — a home-screen view of per-lesson mastery %,
   overall exam history, and a day-streak counter, so progress is visible at
   a glance instead of buried per-lesson.

d. **Nikud (vowel points) Toggle** — a setting to show or hide Hebrew vowel
   pointing, so learners can practice reading unvocalized text as they
   progress.

e. **Boundary** — this is a scoped enhancement pass; no existing v0.1
   behavior outside these four features should change.
